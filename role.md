# Schedule A - Position Duties and Description

## Position Details

- **Position Title:** Senior Fullstack Developer
- **Reports to:** Chief Architect / CTO
- **Location:** Sydney, NSW
- **Employment Type:** Full-time

## Position Overview

The Senior Fullstack Developer will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining web applications and services across both the front-end and back-end layers of the company's technology stack. This role requires technical leadership, strong problem-solving ability, and the capacity to deliver high-quality, scalable, and secure software solutions.

## Key Duties and Responsibilities

### Software Development

- Design, implement, and maintain scalable applications using modern front-end and back-end technologies.
- Develop clean, efficient, and testable code following best practices and coding standards.
- Build responsive, high-performance user interfaces with a focus on usability, accessibility, and modern design principles.
- Implement server-side logic, APIs, and data management solutions to support product requirements.
- Integrate third-party services and APIs as required.

### Architecture and Design

- Contribute to solution and system architecture decisions in collaboration with the Chief Architect and engineering leadership.
- Participate in the design and evolution of microservices, event-driven systems, and cloud-native applications.
- Ensure software is built with maintainability, scalability, and security in mind.

### Collaboration and Leadership

- Work closely with product managers, designers, and other engineers to translate requirements into technical solutions.
- Provide technical mentorship and guidance to mid-level and junior developers.
- Participate in code reviews, architectural discussions, and knowledge-sharing sessions.

### Quality and Delivery

- Develop automated tests (unit, integration, and end-to-end) to ensure reliability and stability of code.
- Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve production issues in a timely manner.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies (stand-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives) and contribute to continuous delivery processes.

### Security and Compliance

- Adhere to secure coding practices and ensure compliance with internal security policies.
- Identify and remediate vulnerabilities within applications and services.
- Contribute to maintaining compliance with relevant standards and regulations (e.g., data protection, privacy requirements).

## Key Skills and Experience

- Proven experience as a Fullstack Developer in a commercial environment, preferably in high-volume or financial/trading systems.
- Strong proficiency in front-end technologies (e.g., JavaScript/TypeScript, React, HTML5, CSS3, WebGL/Canvas).
- Strong proficiency in back-end technologies (e.g., Node.js, REST/GraphQL APIs, databases such as PostgreSQL/MySQL, caching solutions).
- Familiarity with cloud services (AWS or equivalent), containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes), and CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience with observability, logging, and monitoring frameworks (e.g., Grafana, Prometheus, Loki).
- Understanding of secure software development practices.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in a cross-functional team.

## Performance Expectations

- Delivery of high-quality, maintainable, and tested code.
- Contribution to architectural discussions and long-term technical strategy.
- Active participation in peer reviews and mentoring.
- Timely and collaborative communication with stakeholders.
- Alignment of work with business priorities and project timelines.
